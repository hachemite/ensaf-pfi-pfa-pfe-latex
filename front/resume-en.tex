\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

\noindent
This report presents the engineering work conducted during the end-of-year internship (PFA) at \textbf{TechCorp Exemple SARL}. The project focuses on the design and implementation of a highly available microservices architecture dedicated to the distributed management and real-time processing of IoT telemetry streams. To address the critical challenges of horizontal scalability and fault tolerance, a rigorous software engineering methodology was executed.

\vspace{0.4cm}

\noindent
The methodological approach relies on an agile, iterative lifecycle integrating requirements engineering, conceptual architectural modeling, and automated deployment. The resulting technical platform combines a distributed message broker, containerized microservices managed with Docker, and an asynchronous ingestion pipeline engineered to absorb substantial throughput spikes. Experimental results demonstrate a significant reduction in processing latency, high system availability, and reliable event traceability, successfully meeting all requirements defined in the initial specifications.

\vspace{0.8cm}

\noindent
\textbf{Keywords:} Microservices, Internet of Things, Distributed Systems, Real-Time Processing, Software Engineering, High Availability.
